# A small layered section

Open [example.usda](example.usda) to inspect one occurrence inheriting a
20/160/20 mm plaster/masonry/plaster catalog. It needs no facility checkout
or family plugins. The [stable alias](../minimal.usda) remains supported.

Three proxy cubes reveal the layer offsets. This example is useful for
learning catalog inheritance, occurrence overrides and derived-layer muting
in a small stage. Its heights and 0.70 m length are illustrative.

From the repository root, `tools/render_example.py` regenerates the preview;
`--publish` refreshes its separate schema-module illustration. The full
[facility example](../datacentre/README.md) has its own publication command.
